What came out

Each report below is the unedited output of a single run. Nothing was removed because it was inconvenient, and nothing was added by hand.

A disease spectrum fitted as a trajectory, not two arms

GSE135251 · NAFLD liver biopsies · 216 patients · fibrosis stage 0–4

Fibrosis stage is modelled as a slope rather than collapsed into early-versus-late, so no patient is discarded and the monotonicity itself becomes the evidence. 537 genes change consistently across the spectrum, led by LOXL4, LTBP2, ITGBL1 and THY1, with EMT rising and oxidative phosphorylation falling.

Collapsing 216 staged patients into two groups throws away everyone in the middle. Most pipelines make you do exactly that.

A drug effect too small to see gene by gene

GSE334796 · mouse skeletal muscle · 5 animals per arm · TPM, not counts

The matrix is TPM, so the negative-binomial model does not apply and a linear model with variance moderation is used instead. Exactly one gene survives correction. But of the 229 pathways that moved with obesity, 194 move the opposite way under the drug — and 170 of those are no longer distinguishable from lean controls.

This is the honest shape of a small study: underpowered gene by gene, unambiguous at the level of a programme. A report showing only the gene list would have called it a failure.

Response to therapy, predicted before treatment starts

GSE339362 · rectal cancer · 33 pre-treatment biopsies · 17 responders vs 16 non-responders

The hardest of the three. Only three genes clear FDR 0.05 — the honest answer at this sample size. But HALLMARK E2F Targets (NES +2.81) and G2M Checkpoint (+2.72) come out top of the pathway ranking.

Those are the cell-cycle and DNA-repair programs the original authors name in their own title. The pipeline ranked 5,014 gene sets and was told nothing about what to look for.

Working together

Send FASTQ files and they go through STAR and RSEM to a count matrix and on to the finished report. Send a count matrix and it starts there, as the three studies above did. Either way you get quality control with every flag explained in words, the differential expression model with its design written out, MSigDB Hallmark and GO enrichment, co-expression structure with bootstrap support, candidate upstream regulators with a prediction that can be falsified, literature context from Europe PMC, every figure as a vector PDF, full supplementary tables, and a SHA-256 manifest tying every number back to the inputs that produced it.
