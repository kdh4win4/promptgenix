Privacy Policy

What promptgenix.org collects, the third-party services involved, and how to opt out.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This page explains what PromptGenix LLC collects when you visit promptgenix.org, including the notes published at /blog/.

The short version

This is a company site and a technical blog. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, no newsletter, and nothing to log in to. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or anything else about you.

What is collected anyway

Two things happen on almost every website, including this one.

Server logs. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it records requests — your IP address, the page you asked for, your browser's user-agent string, and the time. These logs are GitHub's, kept under their own policy, and we do not have a copy of them.

Measurement and advertising. We may use Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense to see which pages people read and to show ads alongside the posts. These set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser, and they may use them to show you ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites.

If any of those are not yet switched on, nothing is set beyond the server logs above.

Your choices

  • Google lets you turn off personalised advertising at

adssettings.google.com.

  • Third-party vendors' use of cookies for advertising is described at

policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

  • Opting out of a number of other vendors is possible at

optout.aboutads.info.

  • Your browser can block or delete cookies for this site. Nothing here will

stop working if you do.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with its own privacy law, the rights those laws give you — access, deletion, objecting to profiling — apply to the data the services above hold. The quickest route is the links in this section, because that data sits with them rather than with us. You can also write to us and we will help.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not build profiles of readers. We do not run trackers beyond the measurement and advertising described above. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13.

Links out

Posts here link to papers, preprints, and code — bioRxiv, journals, GitHub, and similar. Once you follow a link, that site's policy applies, not this one.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
